About GPA

What is Gyan Prasaran Abhiyan?

GPA is an Abhiyan — a living campaign — to make spiritual knowledge accessible, shareable, research-backed and service-oriented.

Its inspiration is Sri Sri Anandamayi Ma. Its work is not limited to one format: it can become classes, books, digital archives, videos, family learning material, youth programs, public dialogues, volunteer networks and community service.

1

For youth and families: to help the next generation encounter the spiritual riches of India as a source of direction, confidence and inner refinement.

2

For public benefit: to promote education, culture, research, languages, literature, ethics and inclusive community learning without discrimination.

3

For the world: to contribute to a universal conversation on shared spiritual wisdom, harmony and the deeper frontiers of human possibility.

Guiding principles

How GPA wants to serve

The movement is designed around clarity, accessibility, scholarship and seva — so that good intention can become durable public work.

📚

Curricula, teacher training, learning cohorts, workbooks, lectures and accessible resources for all ages.

🪔

Indian languages, Sanskrit, literature, art, philosophy and civilisational memory presented with dignity and care.

🔎

Research

Documentation, citations, archives, timelines and open knowledge practices that help people learn from reliable sources.

🤝

Inclusion

A public-benefit frame that encourages harmony, avoids exclusion and invites sincere participation through seva.

Monthly commitment

How many seva hours can you offer?

Every committed hour can become a story written, a text translated, a class supported, a timeline verified, an archive organized, a youth session hosted, or a family guided toward learning.

Selected commitment: 5 hours per month. Suggested seva: occasional research support, social sharing, translation review or event help.

Skills GPA can use

Research, writing, translation, Sanskrit, design, video editing, social media, event coordination, teaching, fundraising, technology, CRM operations and community moderation.

How GPA will honour your pledge

A monthly seva pledge lets GPA match your capacity to real work, stay in touch responsibly and avoid wasting volunteer goodwill.

Seva with accountability

The aim is not to collect names. The aim is to respect each volunteer’s commitment by engaging them in real work that advances GPA’s mission.

Community
